Customer records a reviewer can check.

Custrel is the place your teams keep accounts, people, and deals. A record can be signed with ML-DSA-87, the signature in CNSA 2.0, so a later review can show it was not changed.

What your teams keep

  • AccountsThe organization you sell to or serve.
  • ContactsPeople at those accounts.
  • LeadsNames that are not a customer yet.
  • OpportunitiesDeals still in progress.
  • CasesProblems a customer asked you to fix.
  • CatalogProducts, price books, campaigns, and tasks tied to that work.

What security can verify

These are the algorithms Custrel uses. They do not replace your own accreditation.

Signing a record ML-DSA-87. ML-DSA-65 is available where a smaller signature is required.
Establishing a key ML-KEM-1024, with ML-KEM-768 where that size is the requirement.
Hashing SHAKE256 by default. SHA-384 can be selected.
Keys that must not be reused XMSS. Each signature advances a counter, so the same key is not used twice.
Passwords A slow hash and a long salt. The password itself is not stored.
The service Requests are rate limited. Browsers are told not to embed the app in another site.
